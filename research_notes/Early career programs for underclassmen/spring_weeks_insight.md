## Exactly who is eligible, and which programs can a student enrolled at an Indian university realistically apply to?

### Takeaway
Only three programs in scope are explicitly built for students at Indian universities: Discover Citadel India (IIT only, open until 2027-02-14), Optiver FutureFocus India (2026 deadlines passed) and IMC Women in Trading and Technology India (IIT/BITS, closed). Beyond those, the best routes are GSA Capital's rolling London insight programmes and Jane Street's FTTP and INSIGHT, whose pages state no university-location limit and fund travel. Most UK bank pages I could read state no location rule at all, so they are "unclear", not "no".

### Cited Findings

**Explicitly open to Indian-university students**
- Discover Citadel Securities India: applicants must be "undergraduate students in good standing, eighteen years of age or older, attending a IIT university with an expected graduation date from Dec 2028 onwards". The event is in Hong Kong at the end of March 2027, the deadline is 2027-02-14 and attendees are told by 2027-02-26 — [Citadel Securities India page, read via search extract only, direct fetch returned 403](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/careers/programs-and-events/discover-citadel-securities/apply-india). **Consulting and India**
- I found no McKinsey, BCG or Bain first or second year insight program for India. Bain CREW is North American, and undergraduate MBB hiring in India concentrates on IIT Bombay, Delhi and Madras, with limited presence elsewhere — [Hacking the Case Interview, third-party](https://www.hackingthecaseinterview.com/pages/consulting-recruiting-india).

### Inferences
- The premise that most spring weeks "need UK or European enrolment" is only partly supported by text. It is explicit at Point72, Optiver, IMC, Citadel EMEA and Maven, but Rothschild, BlackRock and BofA say nothing either way. Their silence is not permission: self-funded travel, a UK visitor visa and a UK-style recruiting funnel still apply, so treat them as long shots and email the recruiter first.
- "UCAS points or equivalent" and "2:1 or equivalent" wording suggests overseas qualifications are not automatically excluded, but no source confirms an Indian applicant has been accepted.
- Realistic priority order for a student at an Indian university:
  1. Discover Citadel India, if at an IIT.
  2. GSA, because it is rolling, so apply soon.
  3. Jane Street FTTP London if a first-year, or INSIGHT Hong Kong if eligible (closes 2026-11-08).
  4. SIG virtual Discovery Event.
  5. Optiver FutureFocus India and IMC Women India in the next cycle.
- Jane Street reportedly asks applicants to use only the location nearest them, which would point an Indian student to Hong Kong, but the Hong Kong FTTP deadline has passed (2026-08-23).

### Gaps
- No source confirms that a student enrolled in India has been accepted to Jane Street FTTP, GSA or a UK bank spring week. Candidates' reports were not found.
- Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, Barclays, Citi, UBS, Deutsche Bank, BNP and Lazard official posting text could not be read, so their location wording is unverified.
- UK visitor-visa rules for attending an insight week were not researched. The visa material I found covers Student visa holders only.
- Citadel's India page disagrees slightly with its general page on the upper graduation bound ("Dec 2028 onwards" versus December 2028 to June 2030).
- The Optiver India extract does not make clear which year group the Nov-Dec 2026 run targeted.
