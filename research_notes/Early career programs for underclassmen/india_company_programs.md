# Company-run programs in India for 1st and 2nd year undergraduates

Research date: 2026-11-06. Batch mapping for a 4-year B.Tech in academic year 2026-27: 1st year = 2030 batch, 2nd year = 2029, 3rd year = 2028, 4th year = 2027.

Field conventions used throughout:
- `years eligible` uses the requested values. "1st and 2nd" means both are accepted, and the program may also accept later years. "2nd only" means 2nd year is the earliest accepted year, and the program may also accept 3rd year. I added one value, "neither", for programs that start at pre-final year or later, and "unclear" where no usable rule was found.
- Where a program opens in May to July, "2nd year" means a student at the end of 2nd year (rising 3rd year). A student in 2nd year today (2029 batch) would be the likely target of the 2027 cycle.
- Everything labelled PROJECTION is extrapolated from past cycles and is not an announcement. `null` means unknown.
- Confidence: high = eligibility and the latest dates seen on an official or official-registration page. Medium = one of those is secondary. Low = aggregators only, or sources conflict.

## Which year of study does each program actually accept?

### Takeaway
Few programs are truly open to 1st-years. In the last cycle, the confirmed ones were The Big Code (Google), Flipkart GRiD, TCS CodeVita, Tata Imagination Challenge and Samsung Solve for Tomorrow, plus first-year-only scholarships from Amazon Future Engineer, Reliance Foundation and Infosys Foundation. A larger group starts at 2nd year, and the rest (including CodeHers, HackerRamp, HackWithInfy and TECHgium) start at pre-final year.

### Inferences
- Batch-based rules roll forward by one year. Girls Wanna Code 8.0, if it runs, should target the 2029 batch, which is today's 2nd-years. Amazon ML Summer School 2027 should target 2028 and 2029. Both are projections.
- Eligibility rules can move sharply between editions. Girls Wanna Code went from 3rd and 4th-years at select women-only colleges ([6.0 on Unstop](https://unstop.com/hackathons/flipkart-girls-wanna-code-60-flipkart-1349594/amp)) to 2nd-years at all engineering colleges (7.0). A monitoring bot should re-read rules each cycle instead of caching them.
- GRiD lets 1st and 2nd-years compete, but the listed hiring outcomes are for 3rd and 4th-years. It is practice and visibility for them, not an internship route.

### Gaps
- Infosys InStep, JPMorgan Code for Good India and Amazon WoW have no verifiable current year-of-study rule.
- Official She++ India eligibility could not be read, because the Eligibility tab is empty in fetched HTML.

## Which programs are discontinued or dormant, and what could and could not be confirmed?

### Takeaway
I found no public company announcement ending any of these programs, so every "dormant" label below is inferred from silence. Microsoft Engage has no verifiable cycle after 2022, Adobe's India scholarship has no information after 2023, and Girl Hackathon, CodeHers, Makeathon, Sparkathon and HackTag each have no 2026 edition I could find.

### Cited Findings
- **Microsoft Engage.** Confirmed: it started in 2020 for second-year students — [IIT Bombay WnCC blog as summarized by search](https://itc.gymkhana.iitb.ac.in/wncc/blogs/ms-engage.html). The 2022 cycle targeted the 2024 batch, with tests on 24 and 27 April, kickoff 4 May and submission 27 to 29 May 2022 — [Engage 2022 page](https://acehacker.com/microsoft/engage2022/).
  - Not confirmed: any 2023, 2024, 2025 or 2026 cycle. An extended search found no registration page or participant account after 2022.
  - The [GeeksforGeeks page](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/?p=1010330) was updated in 2024 but names no later cycle.
  - [PlacementPreps "Engage 2026"](https://www.placementpreps.in/hackathons-2026/microsoft-engage-2026) says registrations "will open soon", but its only link goes to a Google search. Treat it as auto-generated.
  - No Microsoft statement of discontinuation was found. [Talentd](https://www.talentd.in/articles/microsoft-software-engineering-internship) has a heading asking whether it still runs, but search reported that the text does not answer it.
- **Adobe India Women-in-Technology Scholarship.** The aggregator marks the deadline "Discontinued" and says "Adobe has given no information on this scholarship after 2023" — [GyanDhan](https://www.gyandhan.com/scholarships/adobe-india-women-technology-scholarship). The last dated cycle I saw closed 22 August 2021 — [Buddy4Study](https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/adobe-india-women-in-technology-scholarship). I did not reach an Adobe page confirming this. The live Adobe scholarship results are the US program — [Unigo](https://www.unigo.com/scholarships/by-type/scholarships-for-women/adobe-research-women-in-technology-scholarship).
- **Google Girl Hackathon.** Last confirmed edition is 2025. A guide checked 2026-09-07 says no schedule was published — [Thita](https://thita.ai/blog/interview/google-girl-hackathon-2027-preparation-guide). No cancellation notice was found. The Big Code ran in the same part of 2026 and is not women-only, but no source links the two.
- **Walmart CodeHers.** Last confirmed is 2025. The only "2026" page is a third-party one with self-contradicting status — [PlacementPreps](https://www.placementpreps.in/hackathons-2026/walmart-codehers-2026). The usual February to March window passed in 2026 with no listing found.

- **American Express Makeathon.** Last found is 2024 — [HackerEarth](https://www.hackerearth.com/challenges/hackathon/american-express-makeathon-2024/). In 2026, American Express ran CodeStreet for final-years — [Talentd](https://www.talentd.in/articles/american-express-codestreet-2026-hackathon). No source says one replaced the other.
- **Uber HackTag.** Last confirmed is 2.0 in early 2022 — [Uber newsroom](https://www.uber.com/en-IN/newsroom/uber-announces-winners-of-hacktag-2-0-to-unlock-a-world-of-indian-innovations).
- **Goldman Sachs Women Engineers Campus Hiring Program.** The only result was a 2018 edition, per search. No current women-specific Goldman campus program was found.
- **D. E. Shaw Ascend Educare.** Last dated evidence is a 3 September 2023 deadline — [FreshersVoice](https://www.freshersvoice.com/d-e-shaw-off-campus-drive). The D. E. Shaw campus site now shows no fellowships — [campus.deshaw.com](https://campus.deshaw.com/). That site does not cover India, so this proves little.
- **D. E. Shaw Discovery Fellowship.** A US program in New York, not India — [Stanford Women in Business, 2017](https://www.stanfordwomeninbusiness.com/opportunities-blog/2017/2/15/d-e-shaw-discovery-fellowship-317).
- **Samsung PRISM.** No source after 2022 was found, which is a sourcing gap and not evidence of closure. The 2022 plan ran through 2025 — [Samsung Newsroom](https://news.samsung.com/in/samsung-prism-industry-academia-program-successfully-drives-students-to-file-patents-expanding-to-70-engineering-colleges-in-india).

- **Mastercard Campus Code for Change.** The careers URL appeared in search but now returns 404 — [Mastercard](https://careers.mastercard.com/us/en/mastercard-india-campus-code-for-change-hackathon).

### Inferences
- Treat Microsoft Engage and the Adobe India scholarship as paused or discontinued, without calling the ending official.
- CodeHers and Girl Hackathon may have skipped a year or moved dates. Keep watching them and do not retire them.

### Gaps
- No direct Microsoft, Adobe, Google or Walmart statement exists in anything I could reach. Goldman Sachs and Infosys block bots, and Google's program pages are JavaScript shells, so absence on those sites is untested.
